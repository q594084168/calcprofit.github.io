Q: How does the Print on Demand Profit Calculator calculate profit?
A: Net Profit = Revenue − (Product Cost + Shipping + Fee% + Advertising). Profit Margin = Net Profit ÷ Revenue × 100. ROI = Net Profit ÷ Total Cost × 100.
